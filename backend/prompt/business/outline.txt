# Business & Finance Video Outline Extraction Prompt

## Input Format
You will receive a JSON input:

{
  "text": "{video transcript text segment}"
}

- The `text` field contains a segment of the video transcript;
- The content has been manually proofread to ensure basic semantic coherence;
- Use only the input content as your information source — do not perform any external expansion, common-sense reasoning, or logical supplementation.

## Core Task: Balancing Business Value and Practicality
Your task is to **completely cover** all important business topics in the input text, while maintaining **moderate refinement** to avoid over-detailing. The goal is to extract **8-15 core topics**, each corresponding to **3-8 minutes** of video content.

## Topic Extraction Principles

### 1. Business Value-First Principle
- **Practicality first**: Prioritize business viewpoints and analysis most valuable to investors, entrepreneurs, and professionals
- **Data-driven**: Focus on content containing specific data, case analyses, and market trends
- **Avoid empty talk**: Skip purely theoretical discussions; focus on actionable business strategies and practical experience

### 2. Timeliness Awareness Principle
- **Hot topics**: Prioritize content related to current market hotspots, policy changes, and industry dynamics
- **Trend analysis**: Focus on predictions and analyses of future business development trends
- **Timing capture**: Highlight time-sensitive content such as investment timing and business opportunity windows

### 3. Logical Structure Principle
- **Cause and effect**: Ensure each topic contains a complete business logic chain (cause - process - result)
- **Risk-return balance**: Present a balanced discussion of business opportunities and potential risks
- **Multi-dimensional analysis**: Cover different dimensions including macroeconomics, industry analysis, and company fundamentals

### 4. Audience Targeting Principle
- **Tiered value**: Distinguish content suitable for different audience levels (beginner introduction vs professional advancement)
- **Practical guidance**: Prioritize content containing specific operational recommendations and tool recommendations
- **Rich cases**: Focus on real business cases and sharing of success/failure experiences

## Topic Merge and Split Strategies

### Merge Conditions (the following situations should be merged into one topic):
- Different angle analyses of the same company/industry
- Related investment strategy or business model discussions
- Multi-dimensional interpretations of the same economic phenomenon
- Continuous market analysis and prediction content

### Split Conditions (the following situations should be split into different topics):
- Transitioning from macroeconomic analysis to specific company analysis
- Independent discussions of different industries or markets
- Separate discussions of investment strategy and risk management
- Clear distinction between theoretical analysis and practical recommendations

## Output Format

Please output strictly in the following format, without adding any other descriptive text:

### Outline:
1.  **[Primary Topic Name]**
    - [Subtopic 1: highlighting business value and practicality]
    - [Subtopic 2: highlighting business value and practicality]
    - ...

2.  **[Primary Topic Name]**
    - [Subtopic 1]
    - ...

## Output Example

### Outline:
1.  **Current Market Investment Opportunity Analysis**
    - Timing assessment for tech stock valuation recovery
    - Policy dividend interpretation for the new energy sector
    - Key indicator observation for consumer stock recovery
    - Allocation strategies for southbound capital returning to HK stocks

2.  **Startup Valuation and Financing Strategies**
    - Valuation negotiation techniques for Pre-A round financing
    - Key financial metrics investors focus on during due diligence
    - Practical experience balancing equity dilution and control
    - Financing rhythm management at different development stages

3.  **Industry Trends and Business Model Innovation**
    - Analysis of profit model evolution in live-streaming e-commerce
    - Customer acquisition cost optimization for SaaS companies
    - Supply chain management innovation in new retail formats
    - Regulatory compliance strategies for platform economies

## Notes

1. **Business sensitivity**: Ensure extracted topics have clear business value and practicality
2. **Data accuracy**: Focus on content containing specific data, ratios, and time references
3. **Risk reminders**: Present a balanced view of opportunities and risks; avoid one-sided optimism or pessimism
4. **Timeliness assessment**: Prioritize time-sensitive and urgent business information
5. **Audience adaptation**: Consider the understanding abilities and needs of audiences with different business backgrounds
