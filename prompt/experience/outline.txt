# Experience Sharing Video Outline Extraction Prompt

## Input Format
You will receive a JSON input:

{
  "text": "{video transcription text segment}"
}

- The `text` field contains a segment of the video transcription content;
- This content has been manually proofread to ensure basic semantic coherence;
- Please use only the input content as your information source, without any external expansion, common sense reasoning, or logical supplementation.

## Core Task: Balancing Practical Value and Operability
Your task is to **comprehensively cover** all important experiences and techniques in the input text while maintaining **appropriate conciseness** and avoiding excessive detail. The goal is to extract **8-15 core topics**, each corresponding to **3-8 minutes** of video content.

## Topic Extraction Principles

### 1. Practical Value Priority Principle
- **Operability First**: Prioritize extracting experience sharing with clear operational steps and practical techniques
- **Problem-Solving Oriented**: Focus on experiences that can solve specific problems and meet practical needs
- **Avoid Empty Talk**: Skip purely theoretical discussions; focus on replicable, actionable specific experiences

### 2. Experience Completeness Principle
- **Method System**: Ensure each experience sharing contains a complete method system (background-method-effect-notes)
- **Rich Details**: Focus on experience descriptions containing specific details and operational key points
- **Result Verification**: Highlight experience sharing that includes actual results and success cases

### 3. Audience Applicability Principle
- **Reasonable Threshold**: Prioritize experience content suitable for the target audience's ability level
- **Clear Scenarios**: Focus on experiences with clear applicable scenarios and usage conditions
- **Replicability**: Highlight experience methods with universal applicability and easy replicability

### 4. Value Hierarchy Principle
- **Core Skills**: Prioritize experience sharing of key skills and core methods
- **Advanced Techniques**: Focus on advanced techniques that improve efficiency and optimize results
- **Pitfall Avoidance Guide**: Highlight experience lessons that prevent errors and avoid risks

## Topic Merging and Splitting Strategy

### Merging Conditions (should be merged into one topic):
- Different aspects or levels of the same skill or method
- Interrelated operational steps or process stages
- Different solutions or techniques for the same problem
- Different application experiences in similar scenarios

### Splitting Conditions (should be split into different topics):
- Experiences from different skill domains or application scenarios
- Clear distinction between basic skills and advanced techniques
- Solutions for different problem types
- Separation of theoretical learning and practical application

## Output Format

Please output strictly in the following format without adding other descriptive text:

### Outline:
1.  **[Primary Topic Name]**
    - [Subtopic 1: Highlight practicality and operability]
    - [Subtopic 2: Highlight practicality and operability]
    - …

2.  **[Primary Topic Name]**
    - [Subtopic 1]
    - …

3.  **Personal Time Management System Setup**
    - GTD workflow establishment steps and tool selection
    - Priority judgment and time allocation in scheduling
    - Specific methods and psychological adjustment for overcoming procrastination
    - Boundary setting and execution strategies for work-life balance

## Special Considerations for Experience Sharing Content

### 1. Operational Method Experiences:
- Ensure the completeness and logical order of steps
- Include key operational points and notes
- Provide specific time nodes and expected results

### 2. Technique and Method Experiences:
- Clarify the applicable scenarios and usage conditions of techniques
- Include specific operational details and practice points
- Provide effect evaluation and improvement suggestions

### 3. Problem-Solving Experiences:
- Clearly describe the problem identification and analysis process
- Include the basis for solution selection and implementation steps
- Provide effect verification and subsequent optimization methods

### 4. Pitfall Avoidance Experiences:
- Clear identification signs and prevention methods for errors
- Include remedial measures after errors occur
- Provide response strategies for similar situations
