# Business & Finance Video Outline Extraction Prompt

## Input Format
You will receive a JSON input:

{
  "text": "{video transcription text segment}"
}

- The `text` field contains a segment of the video transcription content;
- This content has been manually proofread to ensure basic semantic coherence;
- Please use only the input content as your information source, without any external expansion, common sense reasoning, or logical supplementation.

## Core Task: Balancing Business Value and Practicality
Your task is to **comprehensively cover** all important business topics in the input text while maintaining **appropriate conciseness** and avoiding excessive detail. The goal is to extract **8-15 core topics**, each corresponding to **3-8 minutes** of video content.

## Topic Extraction Principles

### 1. Business Value Priority Principle
- **Practicality First**: Prioritize extracting business perspectives and analysis most valuable to investors, entrepreneurs, and professionals
- **Data-Driven**: Focus on content containing specific data, case analyses, and market trends
- **Avoid Empty Talk**: Skip purely theoretical discussions; focus on actionable business strategies and practical experience

### 2. Timeliness Principle
- **Trending Topics**: Prioritize content related to current market hotspots, policy changes, and industry dynamics
- **Trend Analysis**: Focus on predictions and analysis of future business development trends
- **Timing**: Highlight time-sensitive content such as investment timing and business opportunity windows

### 3. Logical Structure Principle
- **Causal Relationships**: Ensure each topic contains a complete business logic chain (cause-process-result)
- **Risk-Reward Balance**: Balance the presentation of business opportunities and potential risks
- **Multi-Dimensional Analysis**: Cover different dimensions such as macroeconomics, industry analysis, and company fundamentals

### 4. Audience Targeting Principle
- **Tiered Value**: Distinguish content suitable for different audience levels (beginner vs. professional advanced)
- **Practical Guidance**: Prioritize content with specific operational recommendations and tool recommendations
- **Rich Cases**: Focus on real business cases and success/failure experience sharing

## Topic Merging and Splitting Strategy

### Merging Conditions (should be merged into one topic):
- Different angle analyses of the same company/industry
- Related investment strategies or business model discussions
- Multi-dimensional interpretations of the same economic phenomenon
- Continuous market analysis and forecast content

### Splitting Conditions (should be split into different topics):
- Transitions from macroeconomic to specific company analysis
- Independent discussions of different industries or markets
- Separated discussions of investment strategy and risk management
- Clear distinction between theoretical analysis and practical recommendations

## Output Format

Please output strictly in the following format without adding other descriptive text:

### Outline:
1.  **[Primary Topic Name]**
    - [Subtopic 1: Highlight business value and practicality]
    - [Subtopic 2: Highlight business value and practicality]
    - …

2.  **[Primary Topic Name]**
    - [Subtopic 1]
    - …

## Output Example

### Outline:
1.  **Current Market Investment Opportunity Analysis**
    - Timing judgment for tech stock valuation recovery
    - Policy dividend interpretation for the new energy sector
    - Key indicator observation for consumer stock recovery
    - Allocation strategy for capital returning to Hong Kong stocks

2.  **Startup Valuation and Financing Strategy**
    - Valuation negotiation techniques for Pre-A round financing
    - Key financial metrics in investor due diligence
    - Practical experience balancing equity dilution and control
    - Financing pace management at different development stages

3.  **Industry Trends and Business Model Innovation**
    - Profit model evolution analysis of live-streaming e-commerce
    - Customer acquisition cost optimization for SaaS companies
    - Supply chain management innovation in new retail formats
    - Regulatory compliance strategies for the platform economy

## Notes

1. **Business Sensitivity**: Ensure extracted topics have clear business value and practicality
2. **Data Accuracy**: Focus on content containing specific data, ratios, and time nodes
3. **Risk Warnings**: Balance the presentation of opportunities and risks; avoid one-sided optimism or pessimism
4. **Timeliness Judgment**: Prioritize time-sensitive and urgent business information
5. **Audience Adaptation**: Consider the understanding abilities and needs of audiences with different business backgrounds
