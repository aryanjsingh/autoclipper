# Opinion & Commentary Video Outline Extraction Prompt

## Input Format
You will receive a JSON input:

{
  "text": "{video transcription text segment}"
}

- The `text` field contains a segment of the video transcription content;
- This content has been manually proofread to ensure basic semantic coherence;
- Please use only the input content as your information source, without any external expansion, common sense reasoning, or logical supplementation.

## Core Task: Balancing Viewpoint Depth and Critical Thinking Value
Your task is to **comprehensively cover** all important viewpoints and arguments in the input text while maintaining **appropriate conciseness** and avoiding excessive detail. The goal is to extract **8-15 core topics**, each corresponding to **3-8 minutes** of video content.

## Topic Extraction Principles

### 1. Viewpoint Value Priority Principle
- **Uniqueness First**: Prioritize content with unique perspectives, novel viewpoints, or deep thinking
- **Controversy Attention**: Focus on controversial viewpoints with discussion value that can spark critical thinking
- **Logical Completeness**: Ensure each viewpoint contains a complete argumentative logic (viewpoint-evidence-conclusion)

### 2. Critical Thinking Depth Principle
- **Multi-Dimensional Analysis**: Prioritize in-depth discussions with multi-angle analysis and pros/cons comparison
- **Critical Thinking**: Focus on content that demonstrates critical thinking and rational analysis
- **Value Judgment**: Highlight viewpoint expressions with clear value positions and judgment criteria

### 3. Social Significance Principle
- **Real-World Relevance**: Prioritize viewpoints related to current social phenomena and trending events
- **Universal Value**: Focus on viewpoint discussions with universal significance that can evoke resonance
- **Inspirational Significance**: Highlight content with inspirational value and thinking expansion significance for the audience

### 4. Expression Completeness Principle
- **Clear Viewpoint**: Ensure each topic contains a clear viewpoint position and attitude
- **Sufficient Argumentation**: Ensure the completeness of the argumentation process, including facts, logic, emotions, and other multiple forms of argumentation
- **Clear Conclusion**: Ensure viewpoint expressions have clear conclusions or value judgments

## Topic Merging and Splitting Strategy

### Merging Conditions (should be merged into one topic):
- Different levels or angles discussing the same social phenomenon
- Interrelated viewpoint argumentation chains
- Different viewpoint expressions under the same value system
- Multi-dimensional analysis of the same problem

### Splitting Conditions (should be split into different topics):
- Fundamental shift in viewpoint position
- Discussion theme shifts from one domain to another
- Argumentation method shifts from rational analysis to emotional expression
- Obvious change in value judgment criteria

## Output Format

Please output strictly in the following format without adding other descriptive text:

### Outline:
1.  **[Primary Topic Name]**
    - [Subtopic 1: Highlight the uniqueness and critical thinking value of the viewpoint]
    - [Subtopic 2: Highlight the uniqueness and critical thinking value of the viewpoint]
    - …

2.  **[Primary Topic Name]**
    - [Subtopic 1]
    - …

    - Impact of algorithm recommendation mechanisms on thinking patterns
    - Contradictory relationship between information overload and deep thinking ability

    - Significance of rational judgment in the information explosion era

3.  **Deep Reflections on Educational Equity and Social Mobility**
    - Root cause analysis of unequal distribution of educational resources
    - Applicability of "knowledge changes destiny" concept in the new era

    - Strategies for addressing the trend of social class solidification

## Special Considerations for Opinion Content

### 1. Handling Controversial Viewpoints:
- Ensure complete expression of controversial viewpoints without bias
- Include the argumentation process from different positions
- Maintain the original context and emotional tone of viewpoint expression

### 2. Value Judgment Content:
- Clearly distinguish between factual statements and value judgments
- Preserve clear expression of value positions
- Include the basis and standards for value judgments

### 3. Social Criticism Content:
- Maintain the constructive and rational nature of criticism
- Include problem analysis and solution approaches
- Avoid pure emotional venting or meaningless complaints

## Notes

1. **Viewpoint Completeness**: Ensure extracted topics can fully reflect the core position of the original viewpoint
2. **Critical Thinking Value**: Prioritize content with critical thinking value that can spark deep thinking
3. **Social Significance**: Focus on viewpoints with unique insights into social phenomena and inspirational significance
4. **Clear Logic**: Ensure the logical chain of viewpoint expression is complete and the argumentation process is clear
5. **Value Neutrality**: Maintain a neutral stance during the extraction process without making value judgments on viewpoints
