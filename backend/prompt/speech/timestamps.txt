# Speech & Talk Show Video Timestamp Positioning Prompt

## Core Principles: Complete Expression, Emotional Coherence, Effect Preservation

1.  **Expression Completeness**: Timestamps must strictly correspond to complete expression units and content segments.
2.  **Emotional Coherence**: The time range must completely cover the beginning, development, climax, and conclusion of emotional expression, ensuring complete emotional logic.
3.  **Natural Boundaries**: Prioritize positioning timestamps at topic transitions, emotional transitions, or natural boundaries of expression rhythm.

---

## Key Instructions: How to Determine `start_time` and `end_time`

### Determining `start_time`:
-   Should be positioned at the start time of the **first core sentence** of that topic's expression.
-   Ignore irrelevant transitions, pauses, or background music before that content.
-   **Preferred Choice**: Start at the point where the topic is clearly stated or emotional expression begins, ensuring expression completeness.
-   **Special Consideration for Speech & Talk Show**: If there is important buildup, introduction, or atmosphere creation, the start time should be moved earlier accordingly.

### Determining `end_time` (Most Important):
-   **Must be** the end timestamp of the **last sentence** covering the core expression of that topic.
-   **Expression Completeness**: Ensure that core content such as viewpoint exposition, story telling, and punchline presentation is complete without gaps in expression.
-   **Natural Ending**: Prioritize ending at summary statements, after emotional climaxes, or at topic transitions.
-   **Effect Preservation**: If there are important audience reactions, laughter, or applause at the end of the topic, they should be included.
-   If the topic expression discussion continues to the end of the provided SRT text block, then `end_time` should be the end timestamp of the last relevant subtitle.
-   **Wrong Practice**: Mindlessly setting `end_time` to the end time of the entire text block. This must be absolutely avoided.

### Speech & Talk Show Duration Control Principles:
-   **Target Duration**: Each topic segment should be between 3-8 minutes
-   **In-Depth Speech Duration**: Speeches involving deep thinking or complex arguments can be extended to 10 minutes
-   **Exciting Jokes**: Independent complete jokes or punchlines can be compressed to 2-3 minutes
-   **Important Speeches**: Core keynote speeches or important viewpoint expositions can be extended to 12 minutes

### Special Handling for Speech & Talk Show Content:
-   **Complete Jokes**: Complete jokes containing buildup, development, and punchline should be treated as a whole
-   **Emotional Expression**: Important emotional rendering and atmosphere building should be fully preserved
-   **Audience Interaction**: Interactions with the audience, Q&A, and live reactions should appear together
-   **Viewpoint Exposition**: The presentation, argumentation, and summary of core viewpoints should be fully included

---

## Input Format
You will receive a JSON object containing:
-   `outline`: A JSON array containing **multiple** speech topics to process.
-   `srt_text`: A **single** SRT text block related to this batch of topics, formatted as `sequence number\nstart --> end\ntext\n\n`.

## Output Format
-   Strictly following the input outline structure, supplement each topic object with `start_time` and `end_time` fields.
-   **Key**: In the output, rename the input `title` field to `outline`, and rename the `subtopics` field to `content`.
-   Finally output a JSON array containing **all** processed topics.
-   Ensure the time format is `HH:MM:SS,mmm`.

**Strict JSON Output Requirements:**
1. Output must start with `[` and end with `]`; do not add any explanatory text, titles, or Markdown code block markers
2. Use standard English double quotes ", never use Chinese quotes "" or single quotes
3. Ensure all brackets and square brackets match correctly; objects are separated by commas, with no comma after the last object
4. Quotes within strings must be escaped as \"
5. Must not contain any comments, extra text, or control characters
6. Ensure the JSON is completely valid and can be parsed by a standard JSON parser

## Speech & Talk Show Timestamp Positioning Examples

### Correct Timestamp Positioning:
- **Speech Viewpoint**: Starts from "I want to share a viewpoint with you..." and ends with "That's my view"
- **Talk Show Joke**: Starts from the joke's setup and ends with the punchline and audience reaction
- **Story Sharing**: Starts from "Let me tell you a story..." and ends with the story conclusion and reflection
- **Live Interaction**: Starts from the interaction initiation and ends with the interaction completion and response summary

### Errors to Avoid:
- Breaking at the punchline of a joke
- Separating viewpoint exposition from the argumentation process
- Truncating at the climax of emotional expression
- Ignoring important audience reactions or live effects

## Special Considerations for Speech & Talk Show Content

### 1. Speech Content:
- Ensure complete exposition and argumentation of viewpoints
- Preserve important emotional expressions and impactful content
- Include audience interactions and live reactions
- Ensure complete transmission of inspirational content

### 2. Talk Show Content:
- Maintain the completeness and humor effect of jokes
- Include the complete structure of setup, development, and punchline
- Preserve audience laughter and live reactions
- Ensure complete demonstration of humor techniques

### 3. Interview Content:
- Include the complete process of question asking and answering
- Maintain the natural flow and logic of dialogue
- Ensure the completeness of in-depth exchange
- Include important viewpoint collisions and intellectual exchange

### 4. Live Performance Content:
- Preserve the completeness and naturalness of impromptu performance
- Include real-time interaction and feedback with the audience
- Ensure complete demonstration of performance skills
- Maintain the live atmosphere and emotional impact

## Timestamp Positioning Strategies for Different Speech & Talk Show Types

### Keynote Speech:
- Position by viewpoint level: Core viewpoint - Supporting evidence - Summary elevation
- Maintain the completeness of argumentation logic
- Include important emotional rendering and impactful content
- Ensure complete transmission of inspiration and education

### Talk Show Performance:
- Position by joke unit: Independent jokes - Continuous punchlines - Theme connection
- Maintain the completeness of humor effects
- Include audience reactions and live effects
- Maximize entertainment value

### Interview Dialogue:
- Position by topic discussion: Question raising - In-depth exchange - Viewpoint summary
- Maintain the natural flow of dialogue
- Include important viewpoint collisions and intellectual exchange
- Ensure the completeness of information transmission

### Experience Sharing:
- Position by experience stage: Background introduction - Process description - Reflection summary
- Maintain the completeness and emotional impact of the story
- Include important life reflections and inspiration
- Ensure the complete transmission of experience value

## Notes

1. **Expression Completeness**: Ensure each segment contains complete expression logic and content structure
2. **Emotional Coherence**: Important emotional expressions and atmosphere building must be fully included
3. **Effect Preservation**: Punchlines, applause, audience reactions, and other live effects should be treated as important content
4. **Interaction Value**: Exciting live interactions and audience participation should be fully preserved
5. **Inspirational Value**: For speech content, ensure the complete transmission of inspiration and education
6. **Entertainment Effect**: For talk show content, ensure the completeness of humor effects and entertainment value
7. **Authentically Natural**: Maintain the authenticity and live feel of content; avoid over-editing
