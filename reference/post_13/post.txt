Watch expert explains why even trained watchmakers can’t spot the best fake Rolexes anymore 

"the reps right now, they're impossible to tell. literally impossible. a trained watchmaker, just looking with the naked eye, the really good ones, they can't tell"

"the complexity of how many different ways they can screw you is huge. the watch could be real, the parts could be fake, or it could be fake with actual Rolex parts inside"

"you have a yellow gold Daytona, maybe $50K . if it has the green dial, then it's $80K, so they'll take the real Daytona, put a fake green dial on it, pretend it's a John Mayer, and sell it for $30K more"
