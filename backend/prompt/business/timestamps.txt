# Business & Finance Video Timestamp Positioning Prompt

## Core Principles: Precision, Completeness, Clear Business Logic

1.  **Precise positioning**: Timestamps must strictly correspond to core discussion content related to business topics.
2.  **Completeness**: The time range must fully cover all key argumentation points of a business topic, ensuring business logic integrity.
3.  **Natural boundaries**: Prioritize positioning time points at business viewpoint transitions, case switches, or logical paragraph boundaries.

---

## Key Instructions: How to Determine `start_time` and `end_time`

### Determining `start_time`:
-   Should be positioned at the start time of the **first core viewpoint** discussing the business topic.
-   Ignore irrelevant small talk, disclaimers, or transitional phrases before that viewpoint.
-   **Preferred choice**: Start at a business logic boundary to ensure natural topic introduction with clear logic.
-   **Special consideration for business content**: If there is important background setup or data citations, the start time should be moved slightly earlier.

### Determining `end_time` (Most Important):
-   **Must be** the end timestamp of the **last sentence** covering the topic's core business discussion.
-   **Business logic completeness**: Ensure core content such as business analysis, investment advice, and risk warnings is complete with no logical gaps.
-   **Natural ending**: Prioritize ending at summarizing statements, investment advice conclusions, or topic transitions.
-   **Risk warning inclusion**: If there are important risk warnings or disclaimers at the end of the topic, they should be included.
-   If the business topic discussion continues until the end of the provided SRT text block, then `end_time` should be the end timestamp of the last relevant subtitle.
-   **Incorrect approach**: Mindlessly setting `end_time` to the end of the entire text block. This must be absolutely avoided.

### Business Content Duration Control Principles:
-   **Target duration**: Each business topic segment should be between 3-8 minutes
-   **Complex analysis duration**: Complex business topics involving multi-dimensional analysis can be extended up to 10 minutes
-   **Quick news content**: Market briefings, policy interpretations, etc. can be compressed to 2-3 minutes
-   **In-depth analysis**: Deep content such as industry research and company analysis can be extended up to 12 minutes

### Special Handling for Business Content:
-   **Data-intensive paragraphs**: Paragraphs containing large amounts of financial data and market data should be fully preserved
-   **Case analyses**: Complete business case analyses should be kept as a whole and not split
-   **Investment advice**: Investment strategies and risk warnings should appear together and not be separated
-   **Policy interpretation**: Policy content and impact analysis should maintain completeness

---

## Input Format
You will receive a JSON object containing:
-   `outline`: A JSON array containing **multiple** business topics to process.
-   `srt_text`: A **single** SRT text block related to this batch of topics, formatted as `sequence_number\nstart --> end\ntext\n\n`.

## Output Format
-   Strictly follow the structure of the input outline, adding `start_time` and `end_time` fields to **each** topic object.
-   **Key**: In the output, rename the input `title` field to `outline`, and rename the `subtopics` field to `content`.
-   Output a JSON array containing **all** processed topics.
-   Ensure the time format is `HH:MM:SS,mmm`.

**Strict JSON output requirements:**
1. Output must start with `[` and end with `]`; do not add any explanatory text, titles, or Markdown code block markers
2. Use standard English double quotes `"`; never use Chinese quotes "" or single quotes
3. Ensure all brackets and square brackets match correctly, objects are separated by commas, and no trailing comma after the last object
4. Quotes within strings must be escaped as \"
5. Must not contain any comments, extra text, or control characters
6. Ensure the JSON is fully valid and can be parsed by a standard JSON parser

## Business Content Time Positioning Examples

### Correct time positioning:
- **Investment strategy discussion**: From "I think the current market..." to "That's my investment advice"
- **Company analysis**: From "Let's look at this company..." to "Comprehensive evaluation results"
- **Industry trends**: From "The development trend of this industry..." to "Future outlook"

### Errors to avoid:
- Breaking in the middle of financial data explanation
- Separating investment advice from risk warnings
- Cutting at critical argumentation points in case analysis
- Ignoring important market background information

## Notes

1. **Business logic completeness**: Ensure each segment contains complete business analysis logic
2. **Data accuracy**: Important financial data and market data must be fully included
3. **Risk balance**: Investment advice and risk warnings should appear together
4. **Timeliness consideration**: Market briefing content should be handled concisely; in-depth analysis can be extended
5. **Audience adaptation**: Consider the understanding needs of audiences with different investment experience
