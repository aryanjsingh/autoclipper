# Business & Finance Video Timestamp Positioning Prompt

## Core Principles: Precise, Complete, and Clear Business Logic

1.  **Precise Positioning**: Timestamps must strictly correspond to the core discussion content related to the business topic.
2.  **Completeness**: The time range must completely cover all key argumentation points of a business topic, ensuring complete business logic.
3.  **Natural Boundaries**: Prioritize positioning timestamps at transitions of business viewpoints, case switches, or logical paragraph boundaries.

---

## Key Instructions: How to Determine `start_time` and `end_time`

### Determining `start_time`:
-   Should be positioned at the start time of the **first core viewpoint** discussing the business topic.
-   Ignore irrelevant greetings, disclaimers, or transitional phrases before that viewpoint.
-   **Preferred Choice**: Start at a business logic boundary, ensuring natural and logically clear topic introduction.
-   **Special Consideration for Business Content**: If there is important background context or data citation, the start time should be moved earlier accordingly.

### Determining `end_time` (Most Important):
-   **Must be** the end timestamp of the **last sentence** covering the core discussion of that business topic.
-   **Business Logic Completeness**: Ensure that core content such as business analysis, investment advice, and risk warnings is complete without logical gaps.
-   **Natural Ending**: Prioritize ending at summary statements, investment advice conclusions, or topic transitions.
-   **Risk Warning Inclusion**: If there are important risk warnings or disclaimers at the end of the topic, they should be included.
-   If the business topic discussion continues to the end of the provided SRT text block, then `end_time` should be the end timestamp of the last relevant subtitle.
-   **Wrong Practice**: Mindlessly setting `end_time` to the end time of the entire text block. This must be absolutely avoided.

### Business Content Duration Control Principles:
-   **Target Duration**: Each business topic segment should be between 3-8 minutes
-   **Complex Analysis Duration**: Complex business topics involving multi-dimensional analysis can be extended to 10 minutes
-   **Quick News Content**: Market updates, policy interpretations, etc., can be compressed to 2-3 minutes
-   **In-Depth Analysis**: Industry research, company analysis, and other in-depth content can be extended to 12 minutes

### Special Handling for Business Content:
-   **Data-Intensive Paragraphs**: Paragraphs containing large amounts of financial and market data should be fully preserved
-   **Case Analysis**: Complete business case analyses should be treated as a whole and not split
-   **Investment Advice**: Investment strategies and risk warnings should appear together and not be separated
-   **Policy Interpretation**: Policy content and impact analysis should maintain completeness

---

## Input Format
You will receive a JSON object containing:
-   `outline`: A JSON array containing **multiple** business topics to process.
-   `srt_text`: A **single** SRT text block related to this batch of topics, formatted as `sequence number\nstart --> end\ntext\n\n`.

## Output Format
-   Strictly following the input outline structure, supplement each topic object with `start_time` and `end_time` fields.
-   **Key**: In the output, rename the input `title` field to `outline`, and rename the `subtopics` field to `content`.
-   Finally output a JSON array containing **all** processed topics.
-   Ensure the time format is `HH:MM:SS,mmm`.

**Strict JSON Output Requirements:**
1. Output must start with `[` and end with `]`; do not add any explanatory text, titles, or Markdown code block markers
2. Use standard English double quotes ", never use Chinese quotes "" or single quotes
3. Ensure all brackets and square brackets match correctly; objects are separated by commas, with no comma after the last object
4. Quotes within strings must be escaped as \"
5. Must not contain any comments, extra text, or control characters
6. Ensure the JSON is completely valid and can be parsed by a standard JSON parser

## Business Content Timestamp Positioning Examples

### Correct Timestamp Positioning:
- **Investment Strategy Discussion**: Starts from "I think the current market..." and ends with "That's my investment advice"
- **Company Analysis**: Starts from "Let's look at this company..." and ends with "Comprehensive evaluation results"
- **Industry Trends**: Starts from "The development trend of this industry..." and ends with "Future outlook"

### Errors to Avoid:
- Breaking in the middle of financial data explanation
- Separating investment advice from risk warnings
- Truncating at key argumentation points of case analysis
- Ignoring important market background information

## Notes

1. **Business Logic Completeness**: Ensure each segment contains complete business analysis logic
2. **Data Accuracy**: Important financial and market data must be fully included
3. **Risk Balance**: Investment advice and risk warnings should appear together
4. **Timeliness Consideration**: Market update content should be handled compactly; in-depth analysis can be extended accordingly
5. **Audience Adaptation**: Consider the understanding needs of audiences with different investment experience
